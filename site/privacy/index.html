Privacy · updated 26 November 2026

Privacy: what we collect, and what we don't

You don't need an account, we don't set cookies, and your trip search is worked out in your browser: the budget, dates and group size you type are used on your own device to pick and price trips. We don't store them. (Some of our links carry a budget in the web address, such as "Change the budget" on destination pages; that address passes through our host like any page request.)

Who else sees your visit

  • Cloudflare hosts the site. Like any web host, it processes each request (including your IP address) to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces and Wikimedia Commons serves the destination photos, so your browser contacts them too.
  • Partner sites. When you click a partner link (Aviasales, Expedia or a train or bus operator), you leave our site and that partner's privacy policy applies. Affiliate links carry a tag that tells the partner the click came from us and which destination it was for. They don't identify you to us.

Email

If you write to us (for example with the "Was this price right?" link), we use your message and address to reply and to improve our estimates. We don't sell or share them. Ask us at any time to delete what you sent.

There is no mailing list yet. When there is one, it will ask you to confirm by email before sending anything, and every email will have a one-click unsubscribe.

Changes and contact

If this changes (for example, if we start counting visits), we'll update this page and its date first. Questions: hello@farenoughtrips.com.
