Whole trips, priced as one number

Far Enough starts from your budget, not a destination. Type what you want to spend on the whole trip, and it shows the places that fit from Toronto, Montreal or Vancouver: flights or the train, a hotel for every night, and money for food and getting around, added up in Canadian dollars.

How the numbers are made

  • Getting there: return fares other travellers recently saw (Travelpayouts/Aviasales cached fares), plus a margin. For places in reach without flying, a typical train or bus fare, or the cost of driving (fuel and parking).
  • Hotel: the middle of a sample of real hotel rates for the same month and comfort level, adjusted for busy dates.
  • Day-to-day spending: a Canadian baseline scaled by each country's price level (World Bank data).

Every total is a range (low, expected, high), and each part says how fresh it is. In November 2026 we checked 50 sample trips by hand against real prices: the median whole-trip total was within 11%. The method and every trip we checked.

How we make money

We don't sell trips, take bookings or handle payments. Each trip links to a partner site, where you check the live price and book with them directly.

Some of those links are affiliate links: flights on Aviasales (through Travelpayouts) and hotels on Expedia. If you book through them, the partner may pay us a commission, at no extra cost to you. Links to train and bus operators (VIA Rail, GO Transit, Amtrak, BC Ferries Connector) are plain links and earn nothing. Partners don't pay for placement: the order of the results comes only from your budget and our estimates.

Where it stands

This is a prototype, shared with a small group of testers. If a price looked wrong, use the "Was this price right?" link on any trip, or write to hello@farenoughtrips.com. We read everything.
