# $2,500 for two from Toronto or Hamilton (Gemini, 2026-11-26)

**Asked:** "find me trips starting from Toronto or Hamilton, for 2 people with a budget of 2500 cad total in the next few months. The budget includes flight (or other methods of travel), accommodation, and daily spent."
Unlike the ChatGPT test, this prompt gave the group size up front and **asked for non-flight travel explicitly**.

**Tool and setup:** Gemini app, model "Auto", 2026-11-26. Shared link: https://share.gemini.google/02HEoqUQ8V1b (read 2026-11-26; signed out, the Google Maps panel didn't render for me, so the map description is from the owner).

## What it answered

**Format:**
- A **Google Maps "Places" panel** with pins for each destination and the departure airports.
- Three trips, each with a place chip that links to Google Maps.
- Each trip has: transport (for 2), accommodation as a nightly rate, the budget left for spending, and "Best for".
- It closes with clarifying questions: which months, city or warm weather, how many nights, fly or train/drive.

| Trip | Transport for 2 | Lodging | Spending | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Montreal, 3-4 nights, **train or flight** | VIA Rail $350-650 return, or flights $500-800 | $150-230/night | "$900-1,200+ left" | Cafés, Old Montreal, bistros |
| Ottawa, 3-4 nights, **train or drive** | $300-600 by train, or fuel and parking | $120-220/night | "Plenty of room" | Low transport cost, more for food and hotel |
| Cancún, 3-4 nights, **from Hamilton** | $440-520 per person, about $880-1,050+ for two, "before seat/bag add-ons" | Budget hotel or "a modest all-inclusive deal" | "About $1,450-1,620 remaining" for lodging and spending | Escaping winter, direct flight from Hamilton |

## What it means for us

- **Google's edge in chat is Maps, not prices.** Gemini's map and place chips make the answer feel concrete. Our edge is unchanged: consistent sums, dated data, advisories, partner links.
- **Map:** owner's question, my recommendation (feature-ideas #11):
  - **On the results page, yes, as a "List | Map" toggle.** A simple map of Canada and the world, with a dot per place coloured by fit (fits / stretch). It makes "how far your budget goes" literal and helps the launch videos.
  - **On the result card, no map.** People know where Cancún is. Show the route instead: "YYZ → CUN direct, 4h" or "Train 5h from Union". That's what the map was really telling you.
  - **Cost:** draw it as our own SVG (no map tiles, $0, no third-party terms). Google Maps costs money per load past its free tier. The free OpenStreetMap tile server is donation-funded, has a usage policy and can block heavy users ([OSMF policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/)).
- Feature ideas updated: #2, #6, #7, #8, #10. New: #11 map, #12 what's left, #13 all-inclusive.
