# Cancún from Toronto: what a week really costs

*Estimated on 25 November 2026, in Canadian dollars, for 2 adults. Prices change daily; these are estimates, not quotes.*

**Short answer:** a 7-night trip to Cancún for two people, flying from Toronto in late January 2027, comes to about **$2,260 with a basic hotel** or about **$3,600 with a mid-range hotel**, including flights, the room and day-to-day spending. The realistic range is roughly $1,800 to $5,000 depending on how comfortable you want to be and how prices move before you book.

You've probably seen Toronto–Cancún flights advertised for a few hundred dollars. That's real: the cheapest return fare we found for January was $327 per person. But the flight is usually less than half of what the week costs.

Where the numbers come from:

- **Flights:** the cheapest cached return fare for these dates was **$389 per person** (seen on 24 November). Cached fares come from other travellers' recent searches, so the real price when you look may be different. We add a margin: "expected" is that fare plus 10%, "high" plus 35%.
- **Hotel:** per-night prices sampled on 25 November for January stays. The middle figures: about **$82 a night** for a basic room (51 hotels sampled), **$185** for mid-range (33 hotels) and **$650** for upscale (25 hotels). Upscale in Cancún mostly means large resorts, which is why that column jumps.
- **Day-to-day spending:** our estimate for food, local transport and modest entry fees, per adult per day: **$52** basic, **$91** mid-range, **$156** upscale. It's based on Mexico's price level compared with Canada (World Bank data). This is the least checked part of the estimate. If you've just been, [tell us what you actually spent](#).

## What's not included

- **All-inclusive meals and drinks.** These are do-it-yourself prices: a hotel room and you pay for food. All-inclusive resorts bundle food into the room rate, so compare the totals, not the room price.
- **Resort or property fees** some hotels charge on arrival. Our hotel sample shows that many Cancún listings have fees "not included" in the online price. Check the final page before you book.
- **Checked bags**, if your fare doesn't include them.
- **Travel insurance, tours and excursions, tips.**
- **Airport parking or transport to Pearson.**

## Shorter trip, smaller budget

If $2,260 is too much, a 5-night trip (17–22 January) with a basic hotel comes to about **$1,750 for two** (range $1,400–$2,300). With a mid-range hotel it's about $2,730.

## How sure are these numbers?

We check our estimates against Google Flights and Booking.com by hand. In our last spot check (25 November 2026, three trips from Toronto) whole-trip estimates landed within about −8% to +4% of real prices, and every real total fell inside our range. ## Before you go

- **Travel advisory:** the Government of Canada's advisory for Mexico is **"Exercise a high degree of caution" (with regional advisories)**. Read the current advisory at [travel.gc.ca/destinations/mexico](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/mexico) before you book; it covers which areas have stronger warnings.
- **Cash:** if you carry more than US$10,000 (or the equivalent) in cash, cheques or money orders, you have to declare it when entering and leaving Mexico (source: travel.gc.ca, reviewed 23 November 2026).
- **Entry rules depend on your passport.** Check [travel.gc.ca](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/mexico) for current requirements.
