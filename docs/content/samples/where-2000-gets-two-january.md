# Where $2,000 gets two people from Toronto in January 2027

*Estimated on 25 November 2026, in Canadian dollars, for 2 adults sharing a room. Includes return flights, hotel and day-to-day spending. Prices change daily; these are estimates, not quotes.*

> **How we make money:** some links on this page go to partner sites (such as Aviasales for flights and Expedia or Klook for hotels). If you book through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We don't sell trips or take payments: you compare here and book on the partner's site.

**Short answer:** of the 25 destinations we track from Toronto, **three fit $2,000 for two people in January** if you're happy with a basic hotel: **Punta Cana, Cancún and Calgary.** Four more are a stretch. With a mid-range hotel, nothing fits fully.

$2,000 for two is $1,000 each for flights, a room and food. That's tight, so this list is short. We'd rather tell you that than pad it.

## How we worked this out

- **Who:** 2 adults, one room.
- **Trip length:** for each destination, the longest trip (3–14 nights) that fits the budget at the cheapest January dates we had, otherwise the closest.
- **Flights:** cheapest cached return fare for those dates, plus 10% as the expected price (cached fares come from other travellers' recent searches and can be stale).
- **Hotels:** median of a sample of basic-tier hotel prices for January stays, collected 25 November.
- **Day-to-day spending:** an estimate per adult per day from each country's price level compared with Canada (World Bank data). It covers food, local transport and modest entry fees. It's the least checked part of our estimates.
- **Not included:** travel insurance, checked bags if not in the fare, resort or property fees paid at the hotel, tours, tips, getting to Pearson.
- **Accuracy:** in our last spot check (25 November 2026, three trips) whole-trip estimates were within about −8% to +4% of real prices on Google Flights and Booking.com. Holiday and peak dates tend to run low.
- **Entry rules** depend on your passport. Check [travel.gc.ca](https://travel.gc.ca) before you book.
