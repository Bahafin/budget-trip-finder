# Brand and go-to-market

**Summary (2026-11-25)**

**Recommended name shortlist** (owner decides; nothing registered):
1. **Far Enough**: farenough.ca free, farenoughtrips.com free (farenough.com taken), @farenoughtrips appears free on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. No obvious CIPO or USPTO conflict. **Recommended.**
2. **Getaway Math**: getawaymath.com and getawaymath.ca both free, @getawaymath free on Instagram and TikTok (YouTube taken). No obvious conflict.
3. **Trip Kitty**: tripkitty.ca free, thetripkitty.com free (tripkitty.com taken). @tripkitty is taken on Instagram and TikTok (dormant). No obvious conflict.

Tagline: **"See how far your budget goes."** Promise: every trip we show fits your whole budget (flights, hotel and spending money) in CAD, and we tell you how sure we are.

**Top 3 channels** (all $0, time only; ads can't pay back before Gate D):
1. **Short video** (TikTok, Reels, Shorts): "$X for N nights from Toronto: where it actually gets you", made from screen recordings and doable faceless.
2. **Reddit and Facebook travel communities in Canada**: answer real "where can we go for $2,000?" questions with the numbers, within each group's rules.
3. **Email list** with a "tell me when it fits" alert sign-up: the owned channel, and the clearest demand signal.

**Gate C (recommended):** valid at ≥ 1,000 unique visitors. **Pass** = sign-up ≥ 5% of visitors, partner clicks ≥ 15% of searchers, and ≥ 10 unprompted replies. **Fail** = sign-up < 2% and clicks < 8%. Anything in between: change one thing and run 2 more weeks.

**Before going public:** decide how Gate C relates to Gate B (TICO), and check the employer's outside-activity policy (marketing-plan.md §0).
